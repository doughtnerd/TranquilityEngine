# King of Pirates plan

King of Pirates is a king-of-the-hill ship game and the first sample to use ADRs 0067–0074. This plan breaks the engine work and the sample into waves.

## The game

- Up to 8 ships, with bots filling empty slots. One flag: whoever holds it scores a point per second. The highest score when the timer ends wins. Ties are broken by enemy ships sunk.
- Steering: the ship turns toward the mouse's point on the water. A and D pick the port or starboard battery. Space fires (with a cooldown).
- Damage comes from cannonballs, from ramming an obstacle head-on, and from lightning. A sunk ship drops the flag where it sank and respawns after a delay, invulnerable for its first 3 s. A ship that leaves the arena takes damage until it sinks.
- Power-ups, collected by sailing through them:
  - **Wind in your sails:** 5 s of extra speed.
  - **Ghost ship:** 60 s, translucent and glowing. One fog cloud can be dropped (F): it hides ships in it and raises bot path costs through a `NavCostVolume`.
  - **Stormy seas:** 30 s. Higher waves, a darker sky, rain, and lightning that strikes ships other than the collector.
- Arenas: Island Bay, Open Ocean, Rocky Maze.
- Assets are procedural (OBJ and PNG generated by a script in the sample), under the polygon budgets. The owner will replace them later.

Every engine task adds its exports to `src/engine/index.ts`, editors entries where its ADR says, a guide section or page, tests, and its row in `implementation-status.md`. The coordinator merges the branches, resolves conflicts, runs `npm run typecheck` and `npm test`, and reviews.

## Wave 2: the sample

`samples/pirates/`: project, three arena scenes, prefabs, scripts (client and server systems, bots, power-ups, weather), menus and HUD on the UI components, and a lobby with quick match, rooms and bot fill. Built by the coordinator.

## Wave 3: review and polish

A fresh-agent code review of the waves' diff, fixes, an e2e smoke test that the sample loads and plays, and docs.
