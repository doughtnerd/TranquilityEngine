## Rules
- **Write only inside your own folder** `spikes/audio-music/<name>/`. Touch nothing else in the repository (no edits to `src/`, `native/`, `package.json`). **Do not commit.** Use only file, shell and test tools.
- **Cargo:** set `CARGO_TARGET_DIR=/tmp/claude-0/-home-user-TranquilityEngine/29e8b922-4533-5b15-a67e-ee1ae99628d5/scratchpad/cargo-target` for every cargo command (shared, to save disk; cargo locks it, so a wait is normal). Path-depend on `native/audio/crates/*`; copy code into your folder if you must change it.
- **The machine is shared** with other spike agents (4 vCPUs). Timing you take while they compile is unreliable. So: **build, check correctness, and validate your runner with a short smoke run only.** Write a runner (`run.mjs` or `run.sh`) that does the full timed measurement (≥ 5 runs; reports p50, p99, p99.9, max per run, plus the median over runs) and state its expected duration in your reply. **The coordinator runs the timed runs on an idle machine** and sends you the output to finish the README. Correctness checks (bit-exactness, alignment, sample offsets) do not depend on load: complete them yourself.
- **Evidence:** real content (decode real files; the repo has `samples/flappy/music/town.mp3` (MP3), `samples/sound-lab/audio/*.wav`; you may generate test signals; `native/audio/crates/audio-decode/tests/fixtures` / `tests/fixtures/generate.mjs` make FLAC, MP3, Vorbis), output checked for correctness, every unmeasured number marked **(est.)**, every unverified claim **[U]**. On this shared VM only p50 and p99 are trustworthy.
- **Chromium:** Playwright's Chromium is at `/opt/pw-browsers/chromium` (`require('playwright')` works; never run `playwright install`). The container has **no audio device**: use `OfflineAudioContext` or a real `AudioContext` that renders to a null sink; say which.
- **Cite code** as `path:line` for every claim about how the engine works today.
- **Deliverables:** your folder with code, runner and `README.md` (question, method, results tables, findings, unverified list, how to reproduce); a reply under ~500 words with tables, a recommendation, the runner command and its expected duration, and a "what I read" line.
